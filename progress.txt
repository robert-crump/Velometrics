## Issue #241 -- Session Detail: drawer covers floating buttons, thinner border
Completed: 2026-10-05

### What was done

- `PullUpDrawer`: the floating header controls are now composed before the sheet, so the rising sheet
  slides over them. Their `graphicsLayer` alpha is gone (it clipped the circles' contour on the first
  drag pixel), so they're always fully opaque.
- Docked controls: alpha comes from the pure `dockedHeaderAlpha(fraction, coverFraction)`. They stay
  hidden until the sheet's top edge reaches the floating controls' top (status bar + 8 dp) and the last
  `DOCKED_HEADER_FADE_SPAN` = 5 % to full height has begun, then fade to 1 at full height. The floating
  controls are composed exactly while the docked ones are hidden (`dockedHeaderFadeStart`), so one pair
  is always tappable and they never compete for touches. Dragging down reverses the order.
- Floating circle border on Session Detail: 1 dp -> 0.5 dp.
- Tests: `PullUpDrawerHeaderTest` (hidden below the window, 50 % at 97.5 %, opaque at full height,
  fade delayed when the cover point is above 95 %, the window never shrinks to zero width, the hand-off
  between floating and docked).
- Not done: no visual check on a device.

## Issue #233 -- Import: GPS filter for accuracy and acceleration, as in Ride-Graph
Completed: 2026-10-05

### What was done

- The filter moved out of `FitImportServiceImpl` into the pure `GpsQualityFilter` (data/fitimport),
  which returns the kept stream plus discard counts. The service only logs `result.logLine()`.
- New `Datapoint.gpsAccuracyM`, read from the FIT record's `gps_accuracy` (null when absent).
- Accuracy rule: a record with `gpsAccuracyM` > `GPS_MAX_ACCURACY_M` = 15 m is discarded. Files without
  the field are untouched.
- Acceleration rule: implied speed = haversine distance / elapsed time from the last kept record. A record
  whose |Δv| / Δt against the previous kept segment's implied speed exceeds `GPS_MAX_ACCELERATION_MPS2`
  = 5 m/s² is discarded. Gaps over `GPS_ACCELERATION_MAX_GAP_SEC` = 10 s (and zero-time steps) are
  neither checked nor used as the reference speed for the next step. Ride-Graph's `quality_filter.py`
  wasn't available locally, so the semantics follow the issue text.
- Log line: `GPS filter: kept=…, discardZero, discardSpeed, discardPower, discardAccuracy, discardLeap,
  discardImplied, discardAccel`.
- Tests (GpsQualityFilterTest): accuracy 16 m dropped / 15 m kept, missing field, a 10 m/s² GPS jump
  dropped, 4.5 m/s² braking kept, no check across an 11 s gap, a 10 s gap still checked, log counters,
  existing rules.
- DemoRideImportTest: the synthetic demo rides stop at ~7 m/s² and have GPS jitter, so 29/30 lose 1–7
  points (worst 99.84 %). The "no GPS points discarded" check is now `gpsQualityPercent >= 99.5`. The
  generator is unchanged.
- Existing rides change only after delete and re-sync.

`testDebugUnitTest` (560 tests) and `assembleDebug` pass.
