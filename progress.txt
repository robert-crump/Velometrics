## Issue #204 -- Heart rate vs. distance chart on Session Detail
Completed: 2026-09-19

### What was done

- New nullable `cycling_sessions.hrDistanceSeries` JSON column (Room v21, MIGRATION_20_21): 100 `HrDistancePoint`
  (distanceKm, heartRate, altitudeM) per ride, built at import by `HrDistanceSeriesBuilder` (called from
  `SessionMetricsCalculator`). Point k = first record whose cumulative haversine distance reaches k/100 of the ride;
  point 100 = last record. `computeDistance` now shares the builder's cumulative-distance helper, so the chart's
  X axis ends at `distanceKm`.
- Missing HR (or 0) / altitude at a sampled record falls back to the nearest record within +-5 (earlier wins ties),
  else null. Series is null under 1 km (indoor/no GPS) or with no HR at all. No backfill for pre-existing rides.
- `HrDistanceChart` (Canvas): HR line (null breaks it) over a filled elevation backdrop with faint HR zone bands from
  the *current* max HR setting (`SessionDetailViewModel.maxHr`), drag cursor with distance + bpm readout (no altitude).
  Sits at the top of the Heart Rate section, gated on the series existing.
- Tests: `HrDistanceSeriesBuilderTest` (sampling, fallback window/ties, zero HR, thresholds), migration 20->21.

`testDebugUnitTest` and `assembleDebug` pass. No visual check on a device this run.
