## Issue #209 — DropboxSyncWorker runs the Dropbox sync via WorkManager
Completed: 2026-09-19

### What was done

- New `data/dropbox/DropboxSyncWorker.kt` (`@HiltWorker` `CoroutineWorker`): same call sequence as
  `HomeViewModel.syncDropbox()` (connected/reauth checks, `captureBaseline`, `DropboxSyncService.sync()` reused as-is,

  `HomeViewModel` is otherwise unchanged; wiring it to enqueue the worker is #210.
